Scope check: where each method comes from

references/class-scope.md has no Chemistry rows, so every method below comes from the official syllabus in references/syllabus.md.

  • Nernst equation, Faraday's laws, specific and molar conductivity, Kohlrausch's law: Electrochemistry. Solubility product and stability of a complex: Chemical and Ionic Equilibrium, Coordination Compounds.
  • Hydrolysis of salts, buffers, common ion effect, Ksp, polyprotic acids: Chemical and Ionic Equilibrium.
  • Mole concept, redox and neutralisation calculations, limiting reagent, normality: General topics. Graham's law and partial pressures: States of Matter.
  • Raoult's law, ideal solutions: Solutions. Order, half-life, first-order integrated rate law: Chemical Kinetics.
  • Bohr model, de Broglie hypothesis: Atomic Structure.
  • Molecular orbital diagrams up to Ne₂, dipole moment, polarity: Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure.
